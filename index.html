STAY ALERT

Scam Alert Center

Learn about common online scams, recognize warning signs, and protect your money and personal information.

🏦

Fake Bank KYC Messages

High Risk

Fraudsters send messages claiming your bank account will be blocked unless you update your KYC immediately.

Warning Signs

  • Urgent account-blocking threats
  • Unknown links asking for bank details
  • Requests for OTP or PIN

What You Should Do

Do not click suspicious links. Contact your bank using its official website or app.

💸

UPI Payment Scams

High Risk

Scammers may pretend to send you money or trick you into approving a payment request.

Warning Signs

  • Requests to enter your UPI PIN to receive money
  • Unexpected collect requests
  • Pressure to make an immediate payment

What You Should Do

Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money. Check the payment details carefully before approving anything.

📞

Fraudulent Phone Calls

Be Careful

Someone may pretend to be a bank employee, police officer, or government official to scare you.

Warning Signs

  • Threats of arrest or account suspension
  • Requests for OTPs or confidential details
  • Demands for urgent payments

What You Should Do

End the suspicious call. Verify the claim independently through an official contact number.
